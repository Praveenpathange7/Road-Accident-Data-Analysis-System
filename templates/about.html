ABOUT ROADWISE

Better questions from public data.

Roadwise is an educational road-safety analytics project that makes reported accident statistics easier to explore.

◎

Our purpose

Bring State and Union Territory accident counts, national trends, source links, practical safety information, and downloadable reports into one accessible website.

⌁

How it works

Flask serves each page and pandas filters annual data. State/UT accident counts are available from 2015–2024; national totals cover the same ten years. State/UT killed and injured details are bundled for 2017–2023; State/UT casualty detail for 2015–2016 and 2024 is not bundled yet. Latest Data also links to newer official updates.

⌖

Who it can help

Students, researchers, and road-safety teams can explore broad reported patterns and download data for further analysis.

↗

Responsible interpretation

These are aggregate reported counts, not individual crash records or precise locations. Historical reports may use changed State/UT boundaries or revised figures. Counts are not adjusted for population or traffic exposure, and do not prove why accidents happen or predict a specific crash location.

Read each figure in context

Use the source link and reporting year shown on each page. The historical J&K values include Ladakh before the 2019 reorganization.
